Frequently Asked Questions

The Membership
What is concierge medicine?
Concierge medicine is a membership-based model where, instead of billing insurance per visit, patients pay an annual fee for direct access to their physician — longer visits, faster access, and a smaller patient panel so there's real time for you.
What's included in a visit?
Unlimited office visits, extended appointment times (up to 60 minutes), direct access to Dr. Yun between visits, and a comprehensive annual wellness exam. See the Care page for the full list.
Are in-office procedures included?
Your membership includes your physician visits and the time we spend together. In-office procedures — such as joint injections, skin procedures, or cosmetic treatments — are billed separately based on the service provided. Any additional cost will always be discussed with you in advance.
What if I need a specialist?
Dr. Yun coordinates referrals and stays involved in your care alongside any specialist, so nothing falls through the cracks.
What is a Founding Member?
Founding Members are the patients who join the practice in its very first year. As a thank-you for building this practice with Dr. Yun from the very beginning, Founding Members get their annual rate locked in for three full years — even as rates increase for new patients starting in 2027. To qualify, your first annual membership payment must be received by December 31, 2026.
How does membership work?
Membership is billed annually and covers your visits with Dr. Yun directly — there's no per-visit charge and no insurance billing. Current rates:
Adults$3,000 / year
Children ages 10–15$750 / year
Children ages 16–26$1,500 / year
Child memberships accompany a parent membership. Your payment must be received by December 31, 2026 to lock in the Founding Member rate for three years — rates increase in 2027.
Do you accept insurance?
The practice does not bill insurance. Membership covers your visits with Dr. Yun directly. Outside labs, imaging, specialists, and prescriptions are billed by those providers separately, and you're welcome to submit those to your insurance yourself. We recommend keeping a separate insurance plan for hospitalization and specialist care.
Can I use my HSA or FSA?
Just ask, and Dr. Yun will provide an itemized receipt (a "superbill") you can submit to your HSA, FSA, or insurance for possible reimbursement. Whether it's reimbursed depends on your specific plan.
How do I get started?
Schedule a Meet & Greet — a short, no-pressure conversation to see if the practice is the right fit. From there, you'll review and sign a membership agreement and schedule your first visit.
Can I cancel my membership?
Yes, at any time with written notice. You'll receive a prorated refund for the unused portion of your current term.
The Care
Do you prescribe hormone therapy?
Yes. As a Certified Menopause Practitioner, Dr. Yun evaluates and prescribes hormone therapy when it's appropriate, as part of a broader plan that also considers heart, bone, and metabolic health — not hormones in isolation.
Is this practice in-person or virtual?
Visits are primarily virtual for convenience and access, with in-person visits available when your care calls for it.
Do you see the whole family, or just adults?
Both. Membership covers care for every stage of life, and child memberships are available for ages 10–26 alongside a parent's membership.
What happens after hours?
Members can reach Dr. Yun directly by text, call, or email for non-emergency needs. For true emergencies, always call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
Can I get prescription refills without an appointment?
In many cases, yes — just message Dr. Yun directly. No separate visit or portal required for routine refills.
